Your tasks, your way. Pick which lists and calendars TaskFlow uses, or go calendar-free, then plan your day with Top 3, Focus Next and natural-language quick add.

## What's New in This Version

NEW IN 1.15

Make TaskFlow yours
• Choose exactly which reminder lists and calendars TaskFlow uses. Turn off single lists or calendars, or all of Reminders or Calendar. Turned-off items stay in Apple's apps and are hidden from TaskFlow, its widgets and Shortcuts.
• Calendar is now fully optional. Without it, TaskFlow is a focused task and list app, and the Calendar tab appears once you connect a calendar.

Plan your day
• A Plan Your Day card each morning helps you choose your Top 3 and review overdue tasks.
• Choose which lists Focus Next suggests from.
• Upcoming shows every dated task, grouped by day.

Capture and find faster
• Quick add understands dates, times, #tags, !priority, @list and "remind me 30 min before", highlighting each part as you type.
• Ask Siri, "What's due today in TaskFlow?"
• Search covers list details and tags; save and star searches.
• Shopping prices from your history, plus Scan Receipt with on-device text recognition.

Simpler and smoother
• One set of filters, shown as removable chips.
• List, Board and Calendar views; Settings in five clear groups; refreshed themes.
• Undo banners, haptics, animations and clearer empty screens.

Fixes
• Fixed a crash when tapping a task notification.
• Permission screens now say "Continue", so you make the choice in the system prompt.

ALSO NEW SINCE 1.12 (FROM 1.14)
• Notes on the web: read and edit your notes in your browser, synced through your own iCloud.
• Better shopping lists: quick store filters, totals and budgets, Buy Again, and widgets with up to 10 items.
• Improved Watch Later: posters, Continue Watching, season progress and spoiler-free episode screens.
• Guided setup, suggested list layouts and simpler list cleanup.

## Reply to App Review (submission d1808949-cf5c-43b4-b7b2-ac815c8329f6, 1.14 (7))

Hello,

Thank you for the detailed feedback on Guideline 5.1.1(iv).

We've resolved the issue in version 1.15 (build 3), which we have submitted for review. Every button that appears before a system permission request now reads "Continue." This covers Reminders, Calendar, Notifications, Microphone and Speech Recognition, in Settings › Notifications & Permissions, onboarding and in-app prompts. The app no longer uses "Allow …" wording on any button. If access was previously declined, the app shows "Manage in Settings" so the user can change it in the Settings app. The system alert is the only place a user grants or denies access.

We also made Calendar access entirely optional. TaskFlow no longer prompts for Calendar on its main screens. Calendar features appear only after the user chooses to connect a calendar from Settings or during setup.

Please review version 1.15 in place of 1.14. Thank you!

## App Review Information › Notes

1.15 resolves the Guideline 5.1.1(iv) issue from submission d1808949-cf5c-43b4-b7b2-ac815c8329f6. All pre-permission buttons now read "Continue"; once access is denied the app offers "Manage in Settings". To see this, open Settings (gear icon) › Notifications & Permissions.
